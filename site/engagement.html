What the records leave open

The board ranks nothing. Each answer carries the kind of evidence it is, from its source's entry in SOURCES.yml; where a record is used for something else (as a recollection, an inference), its weight is said beside the kind. Two answers in the same words from the same list are one line with both pages: repetition isn't corroboration.
