The sea lanes

The convoys the boats met, on the lanes they sailed: where the war at sea was fought, and how it moved from month to month. A lane's width is the number of convoys on it; the marks are where a Japanese record puts a ship sunk or hit. Every lane between ports is drawn on an assumed route (see the note under the map).

The network

Ports are placed on a modern map; the waypoints between them are assumed, each with its reason in reference/lanes/lanes.yml, until the lanes are traced from the escort divisions' charts, which draw and name them.
