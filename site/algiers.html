Battery C, 62nd Coast Artillery (AA) · No. 153 Squadron RAF · Luftflotte 2

Algiers under the night bombers, May–June 1943

Three evenings when an American anti-aircraft battery four miles west of Algiers fired on raiders: 12 May, 13 May and 4 June 1943. This page puts the battery's morning reports beside the record book of the RAF night fighters flying from Maison Blanche, the city's newspaper, the Allied communiqués, and the attackers' side: the German Naval Staff's war diary and the Italian and German bulletins.

Times are as written. Neither the battery's reports nor the squadron's record book gives a time zone, so a 2050 in one and a 2050 in the other may not be the same minute (see the evidence below). Entries whose time isn't recorded are placed by the order of the account and say time not recorded. The records show the battery firing on these evenings, not what it hit. Every mark on the map and every entry links to the page it was read from; anything placed by assumption is hatched or dashed and says so.

The nights side by side

Every dated entry, by the day it is dated, in three columns: the defenders (the battery, the night fighters, Allied Force HQ), the attackers (the German Naval Staff's war diary, the Italian and German bulletins), and the city's newspaper. Each links to its page.
