Engagements

An engagement is two sides meeting at a place and a time: a pack against a convoy, a destroyer against a kamikaze strike, an antiaircraft battery against a night raid. Each side kept its own records, in its own form and on its own clock. Each page here puts both on one map and one clock, every mark opening the page it was read from; behind it, the questions the records leave open, answered source by source.
