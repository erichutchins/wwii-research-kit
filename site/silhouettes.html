Recognition silhouettes

The aircraft and ships the pages draw, in the manner of the wartime recognition plates. Aircraft are traced from the plates themselves; ships are drawn to the heights and length printed on their ONI 54 plates. Each names its plate. The shapes are data, in records/silhouettes/silhouettes.yml.

Aircraft

Plan view, profile and head-on, each traced by pixel from the plate's solid black silhouette and shown at one scale (span to span). No Hellcat: none of the recognition material read had an F6F plate, and it isn't drawn from memory.

Ships

Profiles, bow to the right, all at one scale, from the lengths and heights printed on the plates. Details smaller than about 4 feet are left out.
