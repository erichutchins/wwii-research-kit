Recognition silhouettes

The aircraft and ships the pages draw, in the manner of the wartime recognition plates. Each is traced from its wartime recognition plate and names it. The shapes are data, in records/silhouettes/silhouettes.yml.

Each comes solid, or detailed: the same shape with the plate's own key lines and small white areas (glazing, windows) knocked out, traced from the plate's pixels inside the outline, never drawn by hand.

Aircraft

Plan view, profile and head-on, each traced by pixel from the plate's solid black silhouette and shown at one scale (span to span).

Ships

Profiles, bow to the right, all at one scale, each traced from its plate and scaled to the length printed there.

Vehicles

Side views, front to the right, all at one scale, traced from the dimensioned side elevations of TM 9-2800 and scaled to the printed length.
