Shelled on the Winter Line: the 67th CA (AA)'s batteries by Venafro and Acquafondata, February 1944

Every entry by the day it is dated, one column per record: the three batteries' morning reports, and the German XIV Panzer Corps' daily reports from the divisions facing them. A German entry is "the same day and nearly the same place" at most: no German report names the batteries. Times are as written; where none is written the entry is placed by the order of the account and says so.

The record's words are in the typed voice, in their own language; the translation under each German entry is ours, in the book voice.
