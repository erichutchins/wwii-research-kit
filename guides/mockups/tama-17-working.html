what we make of it Attack 1 is the torpedoing of Takegawa Maru: the sheet and the log are five minutes and 7.6 nautical miles apart once both are in GMT, within the three navigators' disagreement that night. The FRUPAC note is nineteen minutes later and names a ship not in the convoy's list; it is a candidate, not a match. combinedfleet and JANAC repeat the Japanese record and count once.
