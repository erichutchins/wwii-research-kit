Medical Detachment, 67th Coast Artillery (AA)

What it was. The medical detachment of an anti-aircraft regiment: aid men, a section with the regimental headquarters battery and one with each battalion, on special duty with the gun batteries. It moved with the regiment, and from March 1944 with the 894th AAA AW Battalion, the regiment's 2nd Battalion renamed. (units.csv; the morning reports) The plain words are ours; the designation is the records'.

Where the detachment was, day by day, from its own morning reports to December 1944 and the battalion's printed history for 1945: Paterson, New Jersey to Hüttenfeld, Germany, by way of Oran, Tunisia, the Winter Line, Rome, Siena and the Rhône. Sixty-two changes of station, three sea crossings, five countries after the Atlantic.
